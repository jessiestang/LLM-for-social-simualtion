*Research Topic:*
Simulating the formation of racial segregation patterns in a closed urban environment

*Context Description:*
It is assumed that people prefer a homogenous neighbourhood setting.

*Agent Characteristics:*
There are two types of the agents with different racial identities. 
The number of the two types of agents should be approximately the same.

*Environment and Interactions:*
Agents are arranged in a two-dimensional grid, each interacting with eight neighbours. 
Initially, the agents should be randomly distributed in the grid.
The grid should have about 25%-30% vacant spots that are not occupied by either types of agents

*Behavioral Decisions*:
At each timestep, all agents need to decide if they want to move to a random vacant spot in the grid.
